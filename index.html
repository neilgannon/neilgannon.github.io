Code I'm proud of

Terrain chunks in the open-world project used to hitch the game thread while they generated. I moved the noise sampling onto worker threads with ParallelFor, which cut chunk generation from 38 ms to 6 ms.

  • Rows are independent, so they split across cores without locks
  • Ridged fractal noise gives the sharp crests of wind-blown dunes
  • Every tuning value is exposed to designers through a settings struct
